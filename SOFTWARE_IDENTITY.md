Purpose:

Paradigm Training Manager™ is proprietary software developed exclusively by
Paradigm Strategic Partners, LLC to automate and simplify Texas law
enforcement training compliance management.

The platform is designed to evaluate TCOLE training, certification,
legislative, licensing, and assignment-based requirements and provide
agency leadership with immediate, actionable compliance information.

Product Mission:

Compliance is the outcome.

Product Classification:

Cloud-based Software-as-a-Service compliance management platform.

Primary Market:

Texas law enforcement agencies, including police departments, sheriffs'
offices, constable offices, and other agencies employing TCOLE-regulated
personnel.
